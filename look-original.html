About us

Who is Canterbury Engraving?

Canterbury Engraving has been operating from Christchurch since 1992. We are owner operated and committed to an excellent quality of work.

We use high quality materials and offer a lifetime guarantee, and we work to our customers' time schedules. From screen printing and engraving to wooden signs and brass and stainless steel plaques, every job comes from our Christchurch business on Bamford Street.

We supply the electrical, manufacturing, engineering and toolmaking industries with stainless steel signs and tags, warning signs and certification plates. If you need something identified, labelled or made clearly readable, phone or email us to talk it through.

Materials

Will your signs stand up to New Zealand weather?

Yes. We choose our materials so our signs stay readable outdoors.

Architectural-grade anodised aluminium

The aluminium we use on electrical identification and information signs is anodised to an architectural standard. It is UV resistant and suitable for outdoor use in New Zealand's harsh weather.

Black anodised aluminium & stainless steel

Our black anodised aluminium and stainless steel products will not fade, corrode or disintegrate in the weather, which is why we can back them with a lifetime readability guarantee.

Our guarantee to you

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. We offer a lifetime guarantee, which we call our Lifetime Readability Guarantee.

“Our black anodized aluminium products and our stainless steel products will not fade, corrode or disintegrate due to weather conditions. We guarantee these identification and information signs will remain readable for the lifetime of the post or pole.”
